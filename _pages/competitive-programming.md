I have been involved in competitive programming since junior high school, participating in the **Olympiad in Informatics (OI)** in high school as well as the **International/China Collegiate Programming Contest (ICPC/CCPC)** in college.

At CUHK-Shenzhen, I served as the **student leader** of the university's 30-member competitive programming team from Jun 2024 to Dec 2025, coached by [Prof. Yixiang Fang](https://fangyixiang.github.io/) and [Prof. Chenhao Ma](https://chenhao-ma.github.io/), with additional advice from [Prof. Jingbang Chen](http://chenjb1997.github.io/). During this time, besides contest participation, we continuously provided related resources to the broader university community.

Breaking Barriers
=====
The team and I have been making consistent efforts to address participation gaps in competitive programming, including:
* **Gender inequalities:** Few girls participate in competitive programming contests. I tried to enhance girls' participation in our university. I led a team and won **the 1st place** in [The 11<sup>th</sup> CCPC Girls' Division](https://rl.algoux.org/ranklist/ccpc2025ladies) the first time ever winning this honor in the university's history. Starting from 2023, 4 of 6 girls joining us have won a Gold Medal in ICPC Asian Regionals.
* **Knowledge barriers:** High-level awards in collegiate contests mostly go to students with prior high school OI experience (like me). To broaden access to beginners, I helped build a selection-and-training mechanism and provided **hands-on guidance from scratch** to one of the team where all members had **zero CNOI experience**. They finally won a **Gold Medal** (31st place) in [The 49<sup>th</sup> ICPC Asia Chengdu Regional Contest](https://board.xcpcio.com/icpc/49th/chengdu?group=official).
* **Imbalanced training quality:** Training quality for OI in high schools depends heavily their tiers and resources. Our team provided **weekend lectures** for CUHK-Shenzhen affiliated middle/high schools for OI training (I did a part of them). In 2025, their students achieved 13 prizes in CCF-CSP, with **1 first prize in CSP-S for the first time**, marking a significant breakthrough (see [WeChat post](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/28O-Q5V5HUT9cAIeHFAHdg)).

The team has also been doing other services, including:
* **Voluntary final review lectures** for the following courses on programming, algorithms, discrete math, data structures, and database systems: CSC1001, CSC1003, CSC3001, CSC3100, and CSC3170. I did the overall organization in 2024 and 2025. (WeChat post for [2025](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/ZPVC79dJH5Pw2-A8m0DJ6A), [2024](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/17ouE0bikE4DW-8abUU3fw))
* **CUHK-Shenzhen's annual competitive programming contest.** We set two divisions for students w/ or w/o prior knowledge. We also welcome college and high school students nationwide. 300~500 students join our contest every year. I deeply participated the organization in 2024's contest, and served as the leading student organizer in 2025's. (WeChat post for [2025](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/TYJgMVE0ewP-qykDglk6Ig), [2024](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/HO15ApkFF3ACXIo66RgKEw))
