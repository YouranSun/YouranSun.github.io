<p lang="en">I have been participating competitive programming since junior high school, attending the <strong>Olympiad in Informatics (OI)</strong> in high school as well as the <strong>International/China Collegiate Programming Contest (ICPC/CCPC)</strong> in college.</p>
<p lang="zh">我从初中起接触算法竞赛。在中学时，我参加了<strong>信息学奥林匹克竞赛（OI）</strong>；在大学时，我参加<strong>了国际/中国大学生程序设计竞赛（ICPC/CCPC）</strong>。</p>

<p lang="en">At CUHK-Shenzhen, I served as the <strong>student leader</strong> of the university's 30-member <a href="https://sds-xcpc.github.io/">competitive programming team</a> from Jun 2024 to Dec 2025, coached by <a href="https://fangyixiang.github.io/">Prof. Yixiang Fang</a> and <a href="https://chenhao-ma.github.io/">Prof. Chenhao Ma</a>, with additional advice from <a href="http://chenjb1997.github.io/">Prof. Jingbang Chen</a>. During this time, besides contest participation, we continuously provided related resources to the broader university community.</p>
<p lang="zh">在2024年6月至2025年12月间，我担任了香港中文大学（深圳）30人的<a href="https://sds-xcpc.github.io/">校程序设计竞赛队</a><strong>学生队长</strong>，教练为<a href="https://fangyixiang.github.io/">方一向教授</a>和<a href="https://chenhao-ma.github.io/">马晨昊教授</a>，同时受<a href="http://chenjb1997.github.io/">陈靖邦教授</a>的顾问指导。在这段时间里，除组织队伍参加比赛外，我们还持续为整个大学提供算法竞赛相关资源。</p>

<h1><span lang="en">A Huge Amount of Service</span><span lang="zh">巨量的学生工作</span></h1>

<p lang="en">The team and I have been making consistent efforts to make competitive welcoming to everyone. The purpose of doing this, is to spread helpful knowledge (we believe many ideas from competitive programming are beneficial for undergraduate CS study) and provide a home for folks' interest in this field. We managed to overcome many difficulties, including:</p>
<p lang="zh">整个队伍和我个人，一直在为让算法竞赛更开放，做一些微小但持续的努力。我们希望普及一些有用的知识（因为我们相信算法竞赛的许多理解对本科 CS 学习有帮助），并为对这一领域感兴趣的同学提供一个组织。为此，我们克服了许多困难，包括：</p>

<ul>
  <li lang="en"><strong>Gender exclusion:</strong> Women participate little in competitive programming contests, and I have worked to increase their participation at our university. Since 2023, 6 girls have joined our team, and 4 of them have become gold medalists in ICPC Asia Regional Contests. I also led teams to participate in the CCPC Girls' Division for three consecutive years (2023 – 2025), helping establish it as a tradition at our university. In Fall 2025, we won <strong>1st place</strong> in <a href="https://rl.algoux.org/ranklist/ccpc2025ladies">The 11<sup>th</sup> CCPC Girls' Division</a>, obtaining the first champion in this contest in our university's history (see <a href="https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/FMxGKg2e8ZSot-qS9uJEfg">post</a>).</li>
  <li lang="zh"><strong>性别壁垒：</strong>参加算法竞赛的女生很少，所以我积极在学校推动女生参赛。自2023年起，共有6名女生加入了队伍，其中4人在 ICPC 亚洲区域赛中获得金奖。我连续三年（2023–2025）带队参加 CCPC 女生专场，将其建立为学校和队伍的传统。在2025年秋，我们获得了<a href="https://rl.algoux.org/ranklist/ccpc2025ladies">第十一届 CCPC 女生专场</a>的<strong>全国第一</strong>，在我校历史上首次捧起了该比赛的冠军奖杯（详见<a href="https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/FMxGKg2e8ZSot-qS9uJEfg">推文</a>）。</li>
  <li lang="en"><strong>Knowledge barriers:</strong> High-level awards in collegiate contests mostly go to students with prior high school OI experience (like me). To broaden access to beginners, I helped build a selection-and-training mechanism in our school. In 2024 fall, I formed a team where all members had <strong>zero CNOI experience</strong> and offered them <strong>hands-on guidance from scratch</strong>. They finally won a <strong>Gold Medal</strong> (31st place) in <a href="https://board.xcpcio.com/icpc/49th/chengdu?group=official">The 49<sup>th</sup> ICPC Asia Chengdu Regional Contest</a>.</li>
  <li lang="zh"><strong>知识屏障：</strong>大学算法竞赛中的高端奖项，大多颁给了具备高中 OI 经验的选手（比如我自己）。为让初学者更好参与，我在学校帮助建立了选拔与培训的机制。2024年秋季，我组建了一支三个队员<strong>均没有任何 CNOI 参赛经历</strong>的队伍，并<strong>从零开始手把手地指导</strong>他们。最终，他们在<a href="https://board.xcpcio.com/icpc/49th/chengdu?group=official">第49届 ICPC 亚洲区域赛成都站</a>获得了<strong>金奖</strong>（第31名）。</li>
  <li lang="en"><strong>Imbalanced training quality:</strong> Training quality for OI in high schools depends heavily their tiers and resources. Our team provided <strong>weekend lectures</strong> for CUHK-Shenzhen affiliated middle/high schools for OI training (I did a part of them). In 2025, their students achieved 13 prizes in CCF-CSP, with <strong>1 first prize in CSP-S for the first time</strong>, marking a significant breakthrough (see <a href="https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/28O-Q5V5HUT9cAIeHFAHdg">post</a>).</li>
  <li lang="zh"><strong>资源不均：</strong>高中OI的培训质量，和学校的层次与资源有很大关系。我们队伍每个周末在香港中文大学（深圳）附属学校进行<strong> OI 辅导</strong>（我完成了其中一部分）。2025年，这些学生在 CCF-CSP 中共有13人次获奖，且<strong>首次获得了 CSP-S 一等奖</strong>，实现了从零到一的重大突破（详见<a href="https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/28O-Q5V5HUT9cAIeHFAHdg">推文</a>）。</li>
</ul>

<p lang="en">The team has also been doing other services, including:</p>
<p lang="zh">队伍还做了以下其他服务：</p>

<ul>
  <li lang="en"><strong>Voluntary final review lectures</strong> for the following courses on programming, algorithms, discrete math, data structures, and database systems: CSC1001, CSC1003, CSC3001, CSC3100, and CSC3170, attracting ~800 attendees per year. I did the overall organization for these events in 2024 and 2025. (posts for <a href="https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/ZPVC79dJH5Pw2-A8m0DJ6A">2025</a>, <a href="https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/17ouE0bikE4DW-8abUU3fw">2024</a>)</li>
  <li lang="zh"><strong>无偿期末复习讲座</strong>，涉及程序设计、算法、离散数学、数据结构与数据库系统等专业课（CSC1001、CSC1003、CSC3001、CSC3100、CSC3170），每年有近800人次参加。我在2024年和2025年总负责了这些活动的统筹组织。（推文：<a href="https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/ZPVC79dJH5Pw2-A8m0DJ6A">2025年</a>、<a href="https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/17ouE0bikE4DW-8abUU3fw">2024年</a>）</li>
  <li lang="en"><strong>CUHK-Shenzhen's annual competitive programming contests.</strong> We welcome college and high school students nationwide to attend our contests and set two division for experienced and unexperienced participants. 300~500 students join our contest every year. I deeply participated the contest organization and execution in 2024, and served as the leading student organizer in 2025. Supported by these successful experiences, we held the ICPC China Shenzhen Invitational in 2026. (posts for <a href="https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/TYJgMVE0ewP-qykDglk6Ig">2025</a>, <a href="https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/HO15ApkFF3ACXIo66RgKEw">2024</a>)</li>
  <li lang="zh"><strong>香港中文大学（深圳）年度算法竞赛。</strong>我们欢迎全国高校及高中生参赛，并设有零基础和有基础两个赛道，每年吸引300~500名选手参赛。我深度参与了2024年赛事的组织与执行，并在2025年担任学生总负责人。基于这些成功经验，我们于2026年承办了 ICPC 中国深圳邀请赛。（推文：<a href="https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/TYJgMVE0ewP-qykDglk6Ig">2025年</a>、<a href="https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/HO15ApkFF3ACXIo66RgKEw">2024年</a>）</li>
</ul>
