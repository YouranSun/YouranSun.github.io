Hi, I am Youran, a 3rd-year undergraduate at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen, double majoring in Computer Science and Engineering and Interdisciplinary Data Analytics.

My research interests mainly lie in **LLM-powered data agent and data systems** and **Graph data management**.

> I am now actively seeking a PhD position in Fall 2027!

Experience
======

* I visited **UC Berkeley** in 2025 spring, where I had the privilege of being supervised by **[Prof. Aditya Parameswaran](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~adityagp/)** and gained a enjoyable and rewarding collaboration with [Dr. Sepanta Zeighami](https://szeighami.github.io/) in the [EPIC Data lab](https://epic.berkeley.edu/).
* At **CUHK-Shenzhen**, I was supervised by **[Prof. Yixiang Fang](https://fangyixiang.github.io/)** and had the pleasure working closely with [Dr. Yingli Zhou](https://jaylzhou.github.io/), where I was first introduced to academic research.

Interests
======
My research spans the following topics:
* **LLM-powered data processing:** Improve the data understanding in sematic data processing.
* **Data-driven LLM systems:** Graph RAG prototype system, knob-tuning method for RAG.
* **Graph data management:**  Community search, densest subgraph discovery.
