🏔️ The virtuous loves mountains. / 仁者乐山。
=====
* *The wise love the water; the virtuous love the mountains. (The Analects, Yongye)*
* *智者乐水，仁者乐山。——《论语 · 雍也》*

<!-- I firmly believe people should behave and act morally. If one cannot fully follow it, at least he should not forget it. -->

The Mountain stands for silence, strength and steadfastness. It stands there, unshaken by any force. It has a magnanimous heart and supports the rest through its presence, as another saying goes -- *The Earth’s condition is receptive devotion. The superior man, in accordance with this, carries the outer world with his virtue.（“地势坤，君子以厚德载物。”）*
